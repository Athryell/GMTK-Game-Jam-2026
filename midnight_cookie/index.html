🍪 Midnight Cookie

WASD / arrow keys to move  ·  R to restart
Sneak out for the cookie and get back to bed. Mom does a bed check when the timer hits zero — be in bed, under a table, or behind a wall or you're caught, and each round the timer gets shorter. She roams while you wait: stay out of her cone. Stop moving to hold your breath — she loses your trail — but the breath bar runs out, so you can't hide forever.
